Wild Tropics

Explore the colors, creatures, and hidden wonders of the tropical rainforest.

01. Foundation and Reset

A solid framework ensures that Wild Tropics maintains consistency across different devices. In the reset process, border-box sizing is employed such that the padding and borders are considered in determining the total width of an element, browser default body margin is removed, images are constrained to the container, and font of form controls is the same as that of the document.

02. Design Token Inventory

In Wild Tropics, the principles of reusable design allow for the same look to be created in all the different articles about wildlife and animals. The colors, spacing, typography, and other tokens help organize the design.

Color Tokens

Background: --color-bg: #062F26

Surface: --color-surface: #084B4A

Primary: --color-primary: #159447

Accent: --color-accent: #F47732

Highlight: --color-highlight: #F4C542

Spacing Tokens

Small: 4px | Medium: 12px | Large: 24px | Extra-large: 32px

Sizing Tokens

Content width: 1120px | Reading width: 65ch | Control height: 44px

Corner and Elevation Tokens

Small radius: 6px | Medium radius: 12px | Large radius: 20px

Card shadow: soft shadow around content cards.

Typography Tokens

Heading font: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif

Body font: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif

Small text: 14px | Body text: 16px | Heading text: 32px

Arial and Helvetica are system fonts, so no external font download or font license is required. A sans-serif fallback is provided.
